Privacy Policy & Health Data Protection Protocol

Last updated: November 11, 2026

SECTION 01 / INTRODUCTION & SCOPE

1. Executive Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) sets forth the principles, practices, and protocols governing how Zeldapro Consultancy Private Limited (referred to as “Zeldapro”, “the Company”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, utilizes, processes, protects, stores, transfers, and purges personal and sensitive health data.

This Policy covers our corporate website (zeldapro.com), our proprietary digital health product Carevie (accessible at carevie.in), its iOS and Android mobile applications, backend APIs, client portals, and all associated digital interactions.

This Policy is enacted in strict compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”), the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 (“SPDI Rules”), the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, and the Apple App Store Review Guidelines (specifically Guideline 5.1 on Privacy and Data Collection).

Our Core Privacy Commitment

We believe that health records belong exclusively to the patient. We do not sell user data to brokers, pharmaceutical marketers, advertising networks, or insurance underwriters. All data stored within Carevie is encrypted, access-restricted, and completely within your control.

SECTION 03 / PRODUCT OVERVIEW

3. Carevie Healthcare Product Details

Carevie is a proprietary personal health records (PHR) and medical information management platform developed, owned, maintained, and operated by Zeldapro Consultancy Private Limited.

The Carevie platform allows users to:

  • Digitally Vault Medical Documents: Securely upload, categorize, and organize diagnostic laboratory reports, doctor prescriptions, medical bills, and discharge summaries.
  • Manage Medication Logs: Track ongoing medication dosages, treatment plans, and appointment schedules.
  • Form a Family CareCircle: Securely share selected health records with trusted family members, legal guardians, or emergency caregivers.
  • Generate Temporary Clinical Share Links: Create time-limited, tokenized access links for treating physicians and clinical staff without requiring them to download the app.
  • Emergency Health Card: Maintain voluntary blood group, allergy, and critical medical condition notes accessible during medical emergencies.
  • AI Health Insights: Receive synthesized explanations of complex medical jargon extracted from uploaded test documents.

Crucial Medical Disclaimer

Carevie and Zeldapro Consultancy Private Limited are not licensed healthcare providers, medical clinics, pathology laboratories, or telemedicine practitioners. Carevie does not render clinical diagnoses, medical advice, drug prescriptions, or emergency triage services. All content, document summaries, and AI interpretations are provided solely for informational and personal organizational convenience. Always consult a licensed physician or registered medical specialist for health decisions.

SECTION 04 / LEGAL DEFINITIONS

4. Key Legal & Technical Definitions

  • “Personal Data”: Any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data.
  • “Sensitive Personal Data or Information” (“SPDI”): Personal data comprising medical history, lab reports, physiological records, health documents, biometric authentication tokens, and related records covered under Rule 3 of the SPDI Rules and the DPDP Act.
  • “Data Principal”: The natural person to whom the personal or health data relates (you, the user).
  • “Data Fiduciary”: Zeldapro Consultancy Private Limited, which determines the purpose and means of processing personal data.
  • “CareCircle”: The feature within Carevie permitting users to grant granular, revocable access to designated records to family members or caregivers.
  • “Child Profile”: A sub-profile created and supervised by a registered parent or lawful guardian for a minor below 18 years of age.
  • “Processing”: Any wholly or partly automated operation or set of operations performed on personal data, including collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, adaptation, retrieval, consultation, transmission, dissemination, erasure, or destruction.

SECTION 05 / DATA CATEGORIES

5. Information We Collect

We collect personal and technical information strictly to provide, authenticate, and secure our services. The categories of data collected include:

5.1 Identity & Authentication Data

  • Mobile Phone Number: Used as your primary account identifier and for OTP (One-Time Password) cryptographic verification.
  • Full Legal Name: To personalize your health vault and match uploaded medical reports.
  • Date of Birth & Age: Required to compute age-appropriate health metrics and verify adult user consent status.
  • Gender: Optionally provided for clinical calibration of normal laboratory reference ranges.
  • Email Address: Optional or provided during support/grievance inquiries and transaction confirmations.
  • Profile Photo: Optional image voluntarily uploaded to assist in CareCircle identification.
  • Emergency Contacts: Phone numbers and names of relatives or caregivers voluntarily designated by you.

5.2 Health & Medical Vault Data (Sensitive Personal Data - SPDI)

  • Diagnostic & Lab Test Reports: Blood tests, lipid profiles, imaging reports, scans, and biopsies uploaded as PDFs or photographs.
  • Prescriptions & Invoices: Doctor treatment slips, handwritten or printed prescriptions, and hospital billing receipts.
  • Declared Blood Group: Stored along with voluntary blood report verification documentation.
  • Medication Logs: Drug names, schedules, dosages, and treatment durations entered or extracted from prescriptions.
  • Health Attributes: Voluntary entries regarding allergies, chronic conditions (e.g. hypertension, diabetes), and medical history.

5.3 Technical, Device & Audit Data

  • Device Telemetry: Device model, operating system version (iOS / Android), screen resolution, and language settings.
  • Push Notification Token: Apple APNs or Google FCM device tokens used strictly to deliver transactional updates.
  • IP Address & Network Logs: Recorded during logins and API calls solely for fraud prevention, rate-limiting, and security defense.
  • Audit Trail: Timestamps of file uploads, CareCircle permission changes, and temporary share link creations.

SECTION 07 / COLLECTION METHODS

7. How We Collect Your Data

  • Direct User Input: When you register with your phone number, fill in your profile, take an onboarding survey, or upload health records.
  • Camera & File Uploads: When you use in-app camera or document picker tools to submit medical tests, scans, or prescriptions.
  • Cryptographic OTP Authentication: When you request and verify a 6-digit SMS code delivered via our telecommunications provider (2Factor).
  • Delegated Family Uploads: When an authorized CareCircle family member or parent uploads records to a shared profile.
  • Automated System Logging: Standard web server logs capturing IP addresses, browser user-agents, and API response latencies to ensure platform uptime and security defense.

SECTION 08 / LAWFUL BASIS & PURPOSES

8. Lawful Basis & Purposes of Processing

Under the DPDP Act (Sections 4, 5, and 6) and the SPDI Rules, we process data only under legally recognized grounds:

  • Explicit, Informed Consent: For all Sensitive Personal Data (health documents, lab tests, prescriptions). Consent is requested prior to upload and can be withdrawn at any time.
  • Contractual Necessity: To deliver the Carevie services requested by you under our Terms of Service (e.g. authenticating your login, organizing your vault).
  • Compliance with Legal Obligations: To adhere to statutory orders, tax regulations, and lawful investigative demands from judicial or regulatory authorities.
  • Legitimate Operational Uses: Mitigating cyber security attacks, enforcing API rate-limits, detecting fraudulent multi-account generation, and investigating system anomalies.

PROHIBITED USES: We strictly forbid the use of personal or medical data for targeted behavioral advertising, automated credit risk scoring, health insurance denial, or surveillance.

SECTION 09 / ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE & LLM SERVICES

9. AI Processing & Third-Party LLM Services

OpenAI API Integration

Employed to generate plain-language summaries of complex laboratory test values, defining medical terms and highlighting flagged reference ranges.

Google Gemini API Integration

Employed for optical character recognition (OCR) parsing of doctor handwriting, extracting medication dosages, frequency, and duration to auto-populate your prescription log.

Zero Model Training on Your Health Records

Under these enterprise API agreements, your personal medical data is NOT retained by these providers to train public models. Processing is stateless and strictly transient.

SECTION 12 / MINORS & CHILD DATA

12. Child Profiles & Protection of Minors

Carevie is strictly designed for operation by individuals who are at least 18 years of age. We do not knowingly permit minors to create independent accounts.

Under Section 9 of the DPDP Act 2023, a registered adult parent or lawful legal guardian may create and manage a Child Profile solely to organize the minor child’s pediatric health documents:

  • The parent or legal guardian represents and confirms full legal authority to grant consent for processing the child’s health information.
  • We do not engage in behavioral monitoring, tracking, or targeted advertising directed at child profiles.
  • We do not process any child data in any manner detrimental to the child’s physical or mental well-being.
  • Parents can delete the child profile and all associated medical files permanently at any time.

SECTION 13 / DATA TRANSFERS & HOSTING

13. Cross-Border Data Storage & Transfers

Our primary database infrastructure and medical document stores are hosted with Supabase and DigitalOcean, with primary servers situated to serve our Indian and global user base. When you utilize on-demand AI processing features (OpenAI / Google Gemini), document text is processed via encrypted cross-border API endpoints located in the United States or other jurisdictions adhering to rigorous international security protocols.

Such transfers are permitted under Section 16 of the DPDP Act, and we ensure that all overseas sub-processors are contractually bound to provide levels of data protection at least equal to Indian statutory standards.

SECTION 14 / CYBER SECURITY & SAFEGUARDS

14. Security Safeguards & Encryption

We implement comprehensive technical and organizational security measures meeting or exceeding ISO/IEC 27001 recommendations and Rule 8 of the SPDI Rules:

AES-256 At-Rest Encryption

All stored files, medical scans, PDF reports, and database records are encrypted using Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) 256-bit keys.

TLS 1.3 In-Transit Encryption

All data exchanged between client applications, web browsers, and our APIs is protected by modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).

OTP-Only Authentication

Eliminates weak, reused, or compromised passwords through cryptographically throttled one-time mobile verification tokens.

Principle of Least Privilege

Internal engineers and personnel do not have arbitrary read access to unencrypted patient health records or diagnostic files.

SECTION 15 / RETENTION & APPLE 5.1.1(v) ACCOUNT DELETION

15. Data Retention & Account Deletion

In strict adherence to Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(v) and Section 12 of the DPDP Act 2023, every user has the absolute right to terminate their account and request permanent deletion of all stored health files and credentials.

How to Initiate Self-Service Account Deletion

  1. Within the iOS or Android App: Navigate to Settings → Account Settings → Delete Account.
  2. Confirmation: Confirm your intent. Your account enters an immediate deactivation state.
  3. Via Email Request: Alternatively, submit a deletion request from your registered phone number or email to support@carevie.in or support@zeldapro.com.

Deletion Workflow & 30-Day Security Recovery Grace Period

  1. Immediate Deactivation (Day 0 to 30): Upon requesting deletion, your account is hidden immediately. Active CareCircle links and share tokens are revoked. All push notifications and automated processing are ceased. If you change your mind, you can restore your account within 30 days by authenticating via your registered phone number.
  2. Permanent Cryptographic Purge (Day 31): After 30 days, all personal data, medical records, diagnostic scans, prescriptions, medications, and child profiles are permanently scrubbed from our active database systems.
  3. Minimal Residual Audit Data: A strictly hashed and minimal record (such as account creation/deletion timestamps and fraud-prevention flags) is retained for statutory compliance, anti-fraud prevention, and judicial defense under applicable laws.

SECTION 16 / YOUR STATUTORY RIGHTS

16. Data Principal Rights (DPDP Act & Apple)

As a Data Principal, you are entitled to exercise the following legal rights without hindrance or fee:

Right to Access Summary

Receive a full summary of all personal and health data stored about you and the list of sub-processors with whom it has been shared.

Right to Rectification

Correct, update, or complete any inaccurate or out-of-date personal information in your profile.

Right to Erasure

Request complete permanent erasure of personal data that is no longer required for the purpose it was collected.

Right to Grievance Redressal

Register complaints with our designated Grievance Officer and escalate to the Data Protection Board of India.

Right to Nominate

Nominate a surrogate individual to exercise your privacy rights in the event of death or legal incapacity.

Right to Withdraw Consent

Revoke previously granted consent at any time without impacting processing conducted prior to the withdrawal.

To exercise any statutory right, simply send an email request to privacy@carevie.in or support@zeldapro.com.
