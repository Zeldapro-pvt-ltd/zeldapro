SECTION 01 / INTRODUCTION & SCOPE

1. Executive Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) sets forth the principles, practices, and protocols governing how Zeldapro Consultancy Private Limited (referred to as “Zeldapro”, “the Company”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, utilizes, processes, protects, stores, transfers, and purges personal, enterprise, technical, and product data.

This Policy covers our corporate website (zeldapro.com), enterprise technology consulting engagements, client portals, proprietary digital health platform Carevie (accessible at carevie.in), its web and mobile applications, backend APIs, and all associated digital interactions.

This Policy is enacted in strict compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”), the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 (“SPDI Rules”), the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, applicable global data protection principles (including GDPR standards), and mobile application ecosystem requirements across major application platforms (including the Apple App Store Review Guidelines and Google Play Developer Policies).

Our Core Privacy Commitment

We believe that personal and enterprise data belongs exclusively to the individuals and organizations who entrust it to us. We do not sell user or client data to data brokers, marketing networks, or third-party advertisers. All data stored across Zeldapro’s corporate infrastructure, enterprise solutions, and proprietary products (including Carevie) is encrypted, access-restricted, and under your complete control.

SECTION 03 / ENTERPRISE & PRODUCT SCOPE

3. Enterprise Services & Product Ecosystem (Carevie)

Zeldapro Consultancy Private Limited operates as an enterprise technology, AI solutions, and software engineering company, while also designing, maintaining, and operating proprietary consumer and business products. This Policy governs all facets of our digital footprint:

3.1 Enterprise AI & Technology Consulting Services

Through our corporate presence (zeldapro.com) and direct client partnerships, Zeldapro delivers enterprise artificial intelligence engineering, technology strategy, digital transformation advisory, bespoke cloud architectures, and dedicated software solutions. All enterprise project data, business specifications, and corporate intellectual property are handled under strict non-disclosure, confidentiality, and data protection agreements.

3.2 Proprietary Products: Carevie Digital Health Platform

Carevie (accessible at carevie.in and across web and mobile platforms) is a flagship personal health records (PHR) and health data management platform developed, owned, and operated by Zeldapro. The Carevie platform enables users to:

  • Digitally Vault Medical Documents: Securely upload, categorize, and organize diagnostic laboratory reports, doctor prescriptions, medical bills, and discharge summaries.
  • Manage Medication Logs: Track ongoing medication dosages, treatment plans, and appointment schedules.
  • Form a Family CareCircle: Securely share selected health records with trusted family members, legal guardians, or emergency caregivers.
  • Generate Temporary Clinical Share Links: Create time-limited, tokenized access links for treating physicians and clinical staff without requiring account creation.
  • Emergency Health Card: Maintain voluntary blood group, allergy, and critical medical condition notes accessible during medical emergencies.
  • AI Health Insights: Receive synthesized explanations of complex medical jargon extracted from uploaded test documents.

Crucial Medical Disclaimer

Carevie and Zeldapro Consultancy Private Limited are not licensed healthcare providers, medical clinics, pathology laboratories, or telemedicine practitioners. Carevie does not render clinical diagnoses, medical advice, drug prescriptions, or emergency triage services. All content, document summaries, and AI interpretations are provided solely for informational and personal organizational convenience. Always consult a licensed physician or registered medical specialist for health decisions.

SECTION 04 / LEGAL DEFINITIONS

4. Key Legal & Technical Definitions

  • “Personal Data”: Any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data.
  • “Enterprise & Client Data”: Proprietary business specifications, technical architectures, project briefs, and corporate files processed during technology consulting and engineering engagements.
  • “Sensitive Personal Data or Information” (“SPDI”): Personal data comprising passwords, financial credentials, medical history, lab reports, physiological records, health documents, biometric authentication tokens, and related records covered under Rule 3 of the SPDI Rules and the DPDP Act.
  • “Data Principal”: The natural person or authorized representative to whom the personal or enterprise data relates (you, the client or user).
  • “Data Fiduciary”: Zeldapro Consultancy Private Limited, which determines the purpose and means of processing personal data.
  • “CareCircle & Shared Vaults”: The features permitting users to grant granular, revocable access to designated records to family members, caregivers, or authorized collaborators.
  • “Child Profile”: A sub-profile created and supervised by a registered parent or lawful guardian for a minor below 18 years of age.
  • “Processing”: Any wholly or partly automated operation or set of operations performed on personal or enterprise data, including collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, adaptation, retrieval, consultation, transmission, dissemination, erasure, or destruction.

SECTION 05 / DATA CATEGORIES

5. Information We Collect

We collect personal, technical, and enterprise information strictly to provide, authenticate, and secure our services. The categories of data collected include:

5.1 Identity & Authentication Data

  • Mobile Phone Number: Used as your primary account identifier and for OTP (One-Time Password) cryptographic verification.
  • Full Legal Name: To personalize your health vault, client profile, and match uploaded documentation.
  • Date of Birth & Age: Required to compute age-appropriate health metrics and verify adult user consent status.
  • Gender: Optionally provided for clinical calibration of normal laboratory reference ranges.
  • Email Address: Provided during enterprise inquiries, support/grievance requests, and transaction confirmations.
  • Profile Photo: Optional image voluntarily uploaded to assist in CareCircle or account identification.
  • Emergency Contacts: Phone numbers and names of relatives or caregivers voluntarily designated by you.

5.2 Health & Medical Vault Data (Sensitive Personal Data - SPDI)

  • Diagnostic & Lab Test Reports: Blood tests, lipid profiles, imaging reports, scans, and biopsies uploaded as PDFs or photographs into Carevie.
  • Prescriptions & Invoices: Doctor treatment slips, handwritten or printed prescriptions, and hospital billing receipts.
  • Declared Blood Group: Stored along with voluntary blood report verification documentation.
  • Medication Logs: Drug names, schedules, dosages, and treatment durations entered or extracted from prescriptions.
  • Health Attributes: Voluntary entries regarding allergies, chronic conditions (e.g. hypertension, diabetes), and medical history.

5.3 Technical, Device & Telemetry Data

  • Device & Browser Telemetry: Browser user-agent, device model, operating system version (macOS / Windows / Linux / iOS / Android), screen resolution, and language settings.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Web Push, Apple APNs, or Google FCM device tokens used strictly to deliver transactional updates and health alerts.
  • IP Address & Network Logs: Recorded during logins and API calls solely for fraud prevention, rate-limiting, and security defense.
  • Audit Trail: Timestamps of file uploads, permissions changes, and temporary share link creations.

5.4 Enterprise & Commercial Inquiry Data

  • Consultation Inquiries: Business name, corporate email address, contact phone number, and project scope submitted through zeldapro.com.
  • Client Engagement Records: Technical architecture notes, engagement specifications, and corporate communications governed by enterprise non-disclosure agreements.

SECTION 08 / LAWFUL BASIS & PURPOSES

8. Lawful Basis & Purposes of Processing

Under the DPDP Act (Sections 4, 5, and 6), SPDI Rules, and international privacy standards, we process data only under legally recognized grounds:

  • Explicit, Informed Consent: For sensitive personal data (health records, biometric logins, or survey responses). Consent is freely given, informed, and revocable at any time.
  • Contractual Necessity: To deliver enterprise technology consulting, custom software engineering, and digital product services (including Carevie) under our Terms of Service and commercial client agreements.
  • Compliance with Legal Obligations: To adhere to statutory mandates, corporate audit requirements, tax laws, and lawful investigative orders from judicial authorities.
  • Legitimate Operational & Security Uses: Defending against cyberattacks, mitigating DDoS attempts, enforcing API rate limits, preventing fraudulent accounts, and ensuring continuous platform reliability.

PROHIBITED USES: We strictly forbid the use or sale of personal, health, or enterprise data for targeted behavioral advertising, credit profiling, data brokerage, or third-party surveillance.

SECTION 12 / MINORS & CHILD DATA

12. Child Profiles & Protection of Minors

Carevie is strictly designed for operation by individuals who are at least 18 years of age. We do not knowingly permit minors to create independent accounts.

Under Section 9 of the DPDP Act 2023, a registered adult parent or lawful legal guardian may create and manage a Child Profile solely to organize the minor child’s pediatric health documents:

  • The parent or legal guardian represents and confirms full legal authority to grant consent for processing the child’s health information.
  • We do not engage in behavioral monitoring, tracking, or targeted advertising directed at child profiles.
  • We do not process any child data in any manner detrimental to the child’s physical or mental well-being.
  • Parents can delete the child profile and all associated medical files permanently at any time.

SECTION 14 / CYBER SECURITY & SAFEGUARDS

14. Security Safeguards & Encryption

We implement comprehensive technical and organizational security measures meeting or exceeding ISO/IEC 27001 recommendations and Rule 8 of the SPDI Rules:

AES-256 At-Rest Encryption

All stored files, medical scans, PDF reports, and database records are encrypted using Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) 256-bit keys.

TLS 1.3 In-Transit Encryption

All data exchanged between client applications, web browsers, and our APIs is protected by modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).

OTP-Only Authentication

Eliminates weak, reused, or compromised passwords through cryptographically throttled one-time mobile verification tokens.

Principle of Least Privilege

Internal engineers and personnel do not have arbitrary read access to unencrypted patient health records or diagnostic files.

SECTION 15 / RETENTION & ACCOUNT DELETION

15. Data Retention & Account Deletion

In strict adherence to statutory data protection regulations (including Section 12 of the DPDP Act 2023, GDPR principles, and global consumer privacy laws), every user and client has the absolute right to terminate their account and request permanent deletion of all stored personal, health, and account credentials.

How to Initiate Self-Service Account & Data Deletion

  1. Within Web Portals & Mobile Applications: Navigate to Account Settings → Delete Account (or profile settings).
  2. Confirmation: Confirm your deletion intent. Your account enters an immediate deactivation state.
  3. Via Written Email Request: Alternatively, submit a formal deletion request from your registered email address or phone number to support@zeldapro.com or support@carevie.in.

Deletion Workflow & 30-Day Security Recovery Grace Period

  1. Immediate Deactivation (Day 0 to 30): Upon requesting deletion, your account is hidden immediately. Active share links, CareCircle permissions, and external access tokens are revoked. All notifications and automated processing are ceased. If you change your mind, you can restore your account within 30 days by authenticating with your registered credentials.
  2. Permanent Cryptographic Purge (Day 31): After 30 days, all personal records, diagnostic files, uploaded documents, client drafts, and profile data are permanently and irreversibly scrubbed from active storage.
  3. Minimal Residual Audit Data: A strictly hashed, non-reversible, and minimal ledger entry (such as account creation/deletion timestamps and fraud prevention hashes) is retained only for statutory tax, anti-fraud compliance, and judicial defense under applicable laws.

SECTION 16 / YOUR STATUTORY RIGHTS

16. Data Principal Rights (DPDP Act & Global Standards)

As a Data Principal under the DPDP Act 2023, GDPR, and international data protection standards, you are entitled to exercise the following legal rights without hindrance or fee across all Zeldapro platforms, consulting engagements, and products:

Right to Access Summary

Receive a full summary of all personal and health data stored about you and the list of sub-processors with whom it has been shared.

Right to Rectification

Correct, update, or complete any inaccurate or out-of-date personal information in your profile.

Right to Erasure

Request complete permanent erasure of personal data that is no longer required for the purpose it was collected.

Right to Grievance Redressal

Register complaints with our designated Grievance Officer and escalate to the Data Protection Board of India.

Right to Nominate

Nominate a surrogate individual to exercise your privacy rights in the event of death or legal incapacity.

Right to Withdraw Consent

Revoke previously granted consent at any time without impacting processing conducted prior to the withdrawal.

To exercise any statutory right, simply send an email request to privacy@carevie.in or support@zeldapro.com.
